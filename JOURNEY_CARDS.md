# Journey Card Reference

## Card Types Overview

The pilgrimage to Magi uses three types of cards:

1. **Action Cards** - Your tools for survival and progress
2. **Condition Cards** - Negative effects that clog your deck
3. **Relic Cards** - Powerful permanent effects with pack weight

## Action Cards

Action cards are your primary tools for overcoming challenges. They don't cost anything to play but their effectiveness depends on the situation.

### Hunt
- **Type**: Action
- **Power**: 10-15
- **Description**: Search for food in the wilderness
- **Effect**: Gain sustenance when successful
- **Best Used**: Wilderness nodes, when sustenance is low
- **Deck Variants**: 
  - Hunter's Deck: 15 power (x2)
  - Balanced Deck: 10 power (x1)

### Navigate
- **Type**: Action
- **Power**: 10
- **Description**: Find the best path forward
- **Effect**: Avoid hazards, improve travel outcomes
- **Best Used**: Crossroads, wilderness, uncertain paths
- **Deck Variants**: All starting decks (x1)
- **Notes**: Essential for safe travel

### Bargain
- **Type**: Action
- **Power**: 10-12
- **Description**: Trade goods and negotiate
- **Effect**: Better deals in settlements, successful negotiations
- **Best Used**: Settlement nodes, trader encounters
- **Deck Variants**:
  - Trader's Deck: 12 power (x2)
  - Balanced Deck: 10 power (x1)

### Pray
- **Type**: Action
- **Power**: 10-12
- **Description**: Resist corruption and restore spirit
- **Effect**: Reduce corruption, restore spirit
- **Best Used**: Shrines, when corruption is high
- **Deck Variants**:
  - Faithful's Deck: 12 power (x2)
  - Balanced Deck: 10 power (x1)
- **Notes**: Critical for maintaining purity

### Rest
- **Type**: Action
- **Power**: 8
- **Description**: Recover health and spirit
- **Effect**: Restore health and spirit when at camp
- **Best Used**: Camp nodes
- **Deck Variants**: All starting decks (x1)
- **Cost**: Requires sustenance to be effective

## Condition Cards

Condition cards are acquired through injuries, bad choices, and hardship. They clog your deck and make everything harder.

### Fatigue
- **Type**: Condition
- **Power**: -5
- **Description**: Exhaustion slows your travel
- **Effect**: Reduces travel speed or effectiveness
- **Gained From**: Overencumbered, pushing too hard, lack of rest
- **Removal**: Resting at camps, Amber Reliquary
- **Severity**: Moderate - common but manageable

### Sprained Ankle
- **Type**: Condition
- **Power**: -5
- **Description**: Injury slows movement
- **Effect**: Reduced travel speed
- **Gained From**: Taking risky paths, rushing
- **Removal**: Rest, time, healing
- **Severity**: Moderate - temporary but painful

### Curse Wound
- **Type**: Condition
- **Power**: -2
- **Description**: Slowly drains health
- **Effect**: Health drain over time
- **Gained From**: Exploring ruins, dark magic
- **Removal**: Amber Reliquary, shrines, priests
- **Severity**: High - dangerous if not treated

### Hunger
- **Type**: Condition
- **Power**: -3
- **Description**: Gnawing hunger weakens you
- **Effect**: Increased sustenance drain
- **Gained From**: Low sustenance
- **Removal**: Gaining sustenance above 50
- **Severity**: Moderate - can spiral out of control

### Fever
- **Type**: Condition
- **Power**: -3
- **Description**: Sickness weakens the body
- **Effect**: Health and spirit drain
- **Gained From**: Clay Wastes, disease, poor conditions
- **Removal**: Rest, medicine, shrines
- **Severity**: High - can be deadly

### Corrupted Thought
- **Type**: Condition
- **Power**: -2
- **Description**: Darkness clouds your mind
- **Effect**: Increases corruption gain
- **Gained From**: Corrupted pilgrims, dark choices
- **Removal**: Prayer, shrines, Amber Reliquary
- **Severity**: Very High - leads to bad ending

### Paranoia
- **Type**: Condition
- **Power**: -1
- **Description**: Fear makes everything harder
- **Effect**: Reduced effectiveness of all actions
- **Gained From**: Close calls, dangerous encounters
- **Removal**: Time, positive encounters, rest
- **Severity**: Low - more annoying than dangerous

### Despair
- **Type**: Condition
- **Power**: -4
- **Description**: Loss of hope and faith
- **Effect**: Spirit drain, reduced hand size indirectly
- **Gained From**: Repeated failures, companion loss
- **Removal**: Shrines, helping others, prayer
- **Severity**: Very High - can lead to journey failure

## Relic Cards

Relics are powerful permanent effects that don't go into your deck but provide ongoing benefits. Each relic has a weight value that counts toward your packweight limit.

### Stone of Echoes
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 3
- **Description**: Copy a card played this turn
- **Effect**: Once per node, duplicate the effect of an action card
- **Best For**: Powerful single actions, critical moments
- **Synergy**: Works great with Hunt, Pray, Navigate
- **Rarity**: Rare

### Amber Reliquary
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 2
- **Description**: Remove any condition
- **Effect**: Once per encounter, remove a condition card
- **Best For**: Managing condition buildup
- **Synergy**: Essential for curse management
- **Rarity**: Uncommon

### Pilgrim's Hook
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 4
- **Description**: Pull yourself forward
- **Effect**: +5 bonus progress at each node
- **Best For**: Speed running, catching the Magi
- **Synergy**: Stacks with Traveler's Grace blessing
- **Rarity**: Rare

### Bone Lantern
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 3
- **Description**: Reveals hazards ahead
- **Effect**: See danger before encountering it
- **Best For**: Avoiding bad situations, safe travel
- **Synergy**: Works well with Navigate
- **Rarity**: Uncommon

### Glass Veil
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 1
- **Description**: Avoid one death
- **Effect**: Survive a lethal blow once
- **Best For**: Insurance policy, risky strategies
- **Synergy**: Enables aggressive play
- **Rarity**: Very Rare

### Shrine Token
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 2
- **Description**: Sacred charm from a shrine
- **Effect**: Protects from corruption (+10 resistance)
- **Best For**: Resisting dark choices, maintaining purity
- **Synergy**: Stacks with Divine Protection blessing
- **Rarity**: Common (from shrines)

### Ancient Compass
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 3
- **Description**: Never lose your way
- **Effect**: Navigate actions always succeed
- **Best For**: Exploration, wilderness travel
- **Synergy**: Makes navigation foolproof
- **Rarity**: Rare (from ruins)

### Pilgrim's Staff
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 5
- **Description**: Walking stick of a completed pilgrimage
- **Effect**: +10 health, +5 spirit (passive bonus)
- **Best For**: Durability, extended journeys
- **Synergy**: Good for warriors, long routes
- **Rarity**: Rare

### Water Blessing
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 2
- **Description**: Never thirst
- **Effect**: -2 sustenance drain per day
- **Best For**: Summer, Salt Plains, long journeys
- **Synergy**: Essential for desert travel
- **Rarity**: Uncommon

### Merchant's Scale
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 4
- **Description**: Fair trade guaranteed
- **Effect**: Better deals at settlements
- **Best For**: Merchants, High Road travelers
- **Synergy**: Stacks with Bargain cards
- **Rarity**: Uncommon

### Spirit Anchor
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 2
- **Description**: Holds your soul in place
- **Effect**: Spirit cannot drop below 20
- **Best For**: Preventing death spiral
- **Synergy**: Safety net for spirit management
- **Rarity**: Rare

### Book of Paths
- **Type**: Relic
- **Weight**: 3
- **Description**: Maps and knowledge of the road
- **Effect**: Always know the best path at crossroads
- **Best For**: Optimization, avoiding traps
- **Synergy**: Makes crossroads safe
- **Rarity**: Uncommon

## Deck Building Strategy

### Starting Deck Selection

**Hunter's Deck**: Best for wilderness survival
- Pros: High sustenance generation
- Cons: Weak at settlements
- Recommended Provinces: Fallen Forests, Storm Frontier
- Recommended Season: Winter (food is critical)

**Trader's Deck**: Best for settlement focus
- Pros: Excellent at trading
- Cons: Weak in wilderness
- Recommended Provinces: High Pilgrim Road, Clay Wastes
- Recommended Season: Autumn (many traders)

**Faithful's Deck**: Best for corruption resistance
- Pros: Maintains spirit, resists corruption
- Cons: Limited versatility
- Recommended Provinces: Storm Frontier (high corruption risk)
- Recommended Season: Any

**Balanced Deck**: Best for flexibility
- Pros: Adapts to any situation
- Cons: No specialization
- Recommended Provinces: Any
- Recommended Season: Any (great for first playthrough)

### Managing Your Deck

**Keep It Lean**
- Fewer cards = more consistency
- Skip adding cards when your deck works
- Remove condition cards ASAP

**Card Priority for Removal**
1. Conditions (always remove when possible)
2. Weak duplicate actions
3. Situational cards you never use

**Card Priority for Addition**
1. Relics (powerful but watch weight)
2. Strong actions matching your strategy
3. Versatile actions like Navigate

### Relic Management

**Weight Considerations**
- Base capacity: 20 (varies by background)
- Over capacity = fatigue condition
- Each relic: 1-5 weight typically
- Plan your relic loadout carefully

**Essential Relics**
- Amber Reliquary (condition removal is critical)
- Water Blessing (in Summer/desert)
- Glass Veil (safety net)

**Power Relics**
- Stone of Echoes (doubles powerful actions)
- Pilgrim's Hook (speeds journey)
- Ancient Compass (safe travel)

**When to Skip Relics**
- Pack near capacity
- Relic doesn't fit strategy
- Already have similar effect
- Weight not worth the benefit

## Card Synergies

### Hunt + Stone of Echoes
- Double food gain in one node
- Great for Winter or low sustenance

### Pray + Shrine Token
- Maximum corruption resistance
- Can resist almost any dark choice

### Navigate + Ancient Compass
- Guaranteed safe passage
- Never fail navigation

### Bargain + Merchant's Scale
- Best possible deals
- Turn settlements into resource fountains

### Rest + Pilgrim's Staff
- Enhanced recovery
- Efficient use of camp time

## Counter-Strategies

**Against Condition Buildup**
- Carry Amber Reliquary
- Visit shrines regularly
- Rest at camps before conditions multiply

**Against Corruption**
- Take Pray cards
- Use Shrine Token
- Help others (reduces corruption)
- Visit shrines often

**Against Resource Drain**
- Hunt frequently
- Trade at settlements
- Carry Water Blessing in Summer
- Rest efficiently

**Against Death Spiral**
- Maintain Spirit above 60 (4 card hand)
- Keep one Rest card always
- Use Spirit Anchor as insurance
- Don't let multiple stats get low

## Advanced Combos

**Speed Run Build**
- Pilgrim's Hook relic
- Traveler's Grace blessing
- Salt Plains province
- Summer season
- Navigate-heavy deck

**Tank Build**
- Pilgrim's Staff relic
- Iron Constitution blessing
- Warrior background
- High Road province
- Rest + Pray focused

**Pure Faith Build**
- Shrine Token + Spirit Anchor
- Divine Protection blessing
- Priest background
- Faithful deck
- Visit every shrine

**Resource Master**
- Merchant's Scale + Water Blessing
- Merchant background
- Trader's deck
- High Road (many settlements)
- Trade everything

## Tips for Each Card Type

### Action Cards
- Play the right card for the situation
- Don't hoard - use them
- Diversify your deck
- Match cards to node types

### Condition Cards
- Remove ASAP
- Don't let them accumulate
- Watch for cascading effects
- Prevention better than cure

### Relic Cards
- Quality over quantity
- Watch your weight
- Build around key relics
- Some relics are run-defining

## Conclusion

Your deck is your toolbox for survival. Choose tools wisely, maintain your deck, and remember:
- Actions let you survive
- Conditions kill slowly
- Relics provide power at a cost

The pilgrimage is long. Pack light. Pack smart. Keep faith.

May the Magi guide your path.
