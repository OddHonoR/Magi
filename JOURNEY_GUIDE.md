# Pilgrimage Journey Guide

## Core Premise

You are one soul among many, heading toward the Magi - a holy living city that wanders the world's rim. The city doesn't wait, it doesn't slow down, and you'd better keep pace. Your journey is measured in choices, resources, and faith. Every step matters.

## Starting Your Journey

### Province Selection

Your choice of province determines the terrain you'll face and your base travel speed:

**Salt Plains** (Difficulty: 2/5)
- Fast travel (15 leagues/day base)
- Scarce water sources
- Heat and dehydration risks
- Best for: Experienced pilgrims seeking speed

**Fallen Forests** (Difficulty: 3/5)
- Slow travel (8 leagues/day base)
- Many encounters
- Dense terrain, difficult navigation
- Best for: Those seeking encounters and rewards

**Clay Wastes** (Difficulty: 4/5)
- Moderate travel (10 leagues/day base)
- Hazardous terrain (sinkholes, disease)
- Unpredictable environment
- Best for: Risk-takers wanting challenge

**High Pilgrim Road** (Difficulty: 1/5)
- Safe but long (12 leagues/day base)
- Tolls and settlements
- Protected but expensive
- Best for: First-time pilgrims, cautious travelers

**Storm Frontier** (Difficulty: 5/5)
- Fast but dangerous (13 leagues/day base)
- High risk, high reward
- Extreme weather and hazards
- Best for: Desperate or bold pilgrims

### Season Selection

Seasons modify your journey significantly:

**Spring**
- Moderate conditions
- Beasts awaken and hunt
- Base travel speed unchanged
- Balanced difficulty

**Summer**
- Hot and dry
- Extra sustenance drain (+3/day)
- Faster travel in most provinces (+10%)
- Water becomes critical
- Slower in Salt Plains (-20%)

**Winter**
- Cold and harsh
- Extra sustenance drain (+2/day)
- Slower travel everywhere (-30%)
- Supplies critical for survival
- Hardest season

**Autumn**
- Bandits roam the roads
- Moderate sustenance drain
- Slightly slower travel (-10%)
- Harvest time - easier to find food

### Background Selection

Your background shapes your starting stats:

**Merchant**
- Sustenance: 120 (20% bonus)
- Packweight: 25 (+5 capacity)
- Best for: Long journeys, carrying relics
- Playstyle: Resource management, trading

**Warrior**
- Health: 120 (20% bonus)
- Spirit: 90 (10% penalty)
- Best for: Dangerous routes, combat encounters
- Playstyle: Aggressive, risk-taking

**Priest**
- Spirit: 120 (20% bonus)
- Health: 90 (10% penalty)
- Best for: Resisting corruption, maintaining faith
- Playstyle: Spiritual focus, pure path

**Scholar**
- Spirit: 110 (10% bonus)
- Packweight: 15 (25% penalty)
- Best for: Intelligent choices, minimal relics
- Playstyle: Careful planning, light travel

### Blessing Selection

Choose one divine blessing to aid your journey:

**Traveler's Grace**
- +20% travel speed
- Best for: Long journeys, slow provinces

**Iron Constitution**
- Reduced health drain from low sustenance
- Best for: Winter, difficult terrain

**Divine Protection**
- Reduced spirit loss from hardship
- Best for: Maintaining hand size, resisting corruption

**Light Burden**
- +5 packweight capacity (stacks with Merchant)
- Best for: Relic collectors, item hoarders

### Deck Selection

Your starting deck determines your tools for survival:

**Hunter's Deck**
- 2x Hunt (15 power)
- 1x Navigate (10 power)
- 1x Rest (8 power)
- Best for: Wilderness survival, food gathering

**Trader's Deck**
- 2x Bargain (12 power)
- 1x Navigate (10 power)
- 1x Rest (8 power)
- Best for: Settlements, negotiation

**Faithful's Deck**
- 2x Pray (12 power)
- 1x Navigate (10 power)
- 1x Rest (8 power)
- Best for: Corruption resistance, spirit maintenance

**Balanced Deck**
- 1x Hunt (10 power)
- 1x Navigate (10 power)
- 1x Bargain (10 power)
- 1x Pray (10 power)
- 1x Rest (8 power)
- Best for: Versatility, adapting to any situation

## Core Stats

### Sustenance (0-100)
Your food and water supply. Critical for survival.
- Drains 5-10 per day depending on season
- Below 20: Health starts draining (-3/day)
- At 0: Rapid health loss
- Restored: At settlements, hunting successfully, resting at camps

### Health (0-100)
Your physical wellbeing. Damage comes from encounters and starvation.
- At 0: Death, journey ends
- Affects stamina and ability to handle challenges
- Below 30: Spirit starts draining (-2/day)
- Restored: Resting at camps, settling down, some encounters

### Spirit (0-100)
Your faith, resolve, and will to continue. Most important stat.
- Determines hand size:
  - 80-100: Draw 5 cards
  - 60-79: Draw 4 cards
  - 40-59: Draw 3 cards
  - 20-39: Draw 2 cards
  - 0-19: Draw 1 card
- At 0: Spiritual death, journey ends
- Lost: Through corruption, hardship, moral compromise
- Restored: At shrines, through prayer, helping others

### Packweight (0-20+)
How much you're carrying. Affects your mobility.
- Maximum determined by background and blessings
- Relics take up weight
- Exceeding maximum causes fatigue
- Lighter pack = more agile decisions

### Progress (0-1000)
Distance traveled toward the Magi.
- Goal: Reach 1000 leagues
- Progress varies by province and season
- Modified by blessings and conditions
- Race against resource depletion

### Hidden Stats

**Corruption**
- Accumulates from dark choices
- High corruption leads to bad ending
- Reduced through prayer, shrines, helping others

**Companions**
- Fellow pilgrims who travel with you
- Affect final outcome
- Can be gained through helping others

## Node Types

### Camp
A place to rest and recover.

Choices:
- Rest and recover (costs sustenance, restores health/spirit)
- Craft and prepare (costs sustenance, adds action card)
- Push on without rest (no cost, no benefit)

Strategy: Use camps when low on health/spirit, but conserve sustenance.

### Crossroads
Choose your path forward.

Choices:
- Well-worn path (safer, slower, +5 progress)
- Overgrown trail (faster, riskier, +15 progress, injury chance)

Strategy: Take risks when healthy, play safe when injured.

### Shrine
Holy places offering blessings or tests.

Choices:
- Pray at shrine (costs spirit, restores spirit, reduces corruption)
- Search for relics (chance to find relic or nothing)
- Move on respectfully (no cost, no benefit)

Strategy: Shrines are crucial for corruption management.

### Settlement
Trade posts and rest stops.

Choices:
- Trade for supplies (costs progress, gains sustenance)
- Hire protection (costs progress, gains companion)
- Listen to gossip (free spirit boost)
- Move on quickly (no cost, no benefit)

Strategy: Balance progress vs. resources carefully.

### Wilderness
Dangerous and unpredictable.

Encounters:
- Beast attacks (health loss, sustenance loss)
- Hermits (sharing costs sustenance, gains spirit)
- Lost caravans (helping costs time/health, gains sustenance)
- Corrupted pilgrims (resist with spirit, fight with health, or flee)
- Foraging (gain sustenance)

Strategy: Most dangerous node type - be prepared.

### Ruins
Ancient places with treasures and curses.

Choices:
- Explore carefully (chance of relic or trap/curse)
- Loot quickly (guaranteed sustenance, corruption risk)
- Avoid the ruins (no cost, no benefit)

Strategy: Risk vs. reward - relics are powerful but curses are dangerous.

## Card Types

### Action Cards
Your tools for survival. Play these to overcome challenges.

Examples:
- **Hunt**: Gain sustenance from wilderness
- **Navigate**: Find better paths, avoid hazards
- **Bargain**: Trade effectively, negotiate better deals
- **Pray**: Resist corruption, restore spirit
- **Rest**: Recover health and spirit

### Condition Cards
Bad cards that clog your deck. Acquired through injuries, curses, and hardship.

Examples:
- **Fatigue**: Slows travel
- **Injury**: Reduces effectiveness
- **Curse**: Various negative effects
- **Hunger**: Increases sustenance drain
- **Fever**: Health drain over time

Strategy: Minimize condition cards - they make everything harder.

### Relic Cards
Powerful permanent effects, but take up pack weight.

Examples:
- **Stone of Echoes**: Copy a card played this turn
- **Amber Reliquary**: Remove any condition
- **Pilgrim's Hook**: Extra progress at nodes
- **Bone Lantern**: Reveals hazards ahead
- **Glass Veil**: Avoid one death

Strategy: Choose relics carefully - weight matters.

## Encounters

### Beasts
- Wolves, predators, dangerous wildlife
- Usually costs health and sustenance
- Can fight or flee
- More common in Spring

### Hermits
- Lone travelers, wise souls
- Often offer wisdom for supplies
- Sharing gains spirit
- Keeping sustenance increases corruption

### Lost Caravans
- Other pilgrims in trouble
- Helping costs time/health
- Rewards sustenance and karma
- Ignoring increases corruption

### Corrupted Pilgrims
- Those who fell to darkness
- Dangerous and demanding
- Resist with spirit or fight with health
- Fleeing costs progress

### Traders
- Merchants on the road
- Trade progress for supplies
- Trade supplies for relics
- Fair or unfair deals

### Agents of the Magi
- Rare encounters
- Test your worthiness
- Can provide major benefits
- Can curse the unworthy

## Strategy Tips

### Resource Management
1. Keep sustenance above 20 to avoid health drain
2. Keep health above 30 to avoid spirit drain
3. Prioritize spirit - it determines hand size
4. Don't hoard relics beyond capacity

### Deck Building
1. Keep deck lean - fewer cards means consistency
2. Remove condition cards when possible
3. Balance different action types
4. Relics are powerful but heavy

### Route Planning
1. Faster provinces = less time for resource drain
2. Safer provinces = fewer dangerous encounters
3. Season affects everything - plan accordingly
4. Settlements can save you - don't skip them all

### Corruption Management
1. Avoid dark choices when possible
2. Use shrines to reduce corruption
3. Help others to stay pure
4. High corruption leads to bad ending

### Spirit Maintenance
1. Spirit is your most important stat
2. Low spirit = fewer cards = fewer options = death spiral
3. Pray regularly, visit shrines, help others
4. Never let spirit drop too low

## Endings

### Blessed (Best Ending)
**Requirements**: Spirit ≥70, Corruption <20
- Welcomed into the Magi
- Your pilgrimage is successful
- You receive the blessing you sought

### Turned Away
**Requirements**: Spirit ≤20
- Your spirit is too broken to enter
- Sent away to recover and try again
- Not the worst fate, but not success

### Consumed (Worst Ending)
**Requirements**: Corruption ≥50
- Darkness overtakes you
- Lost to corruption
- Become a shadow on the road

### Outer Wall (Neutral Ending)
**Requirements**: Mixed stats
- Neither blessed nor corrupted enough
- Become part of the city's defenses
- Not what you sought, but an ending nonetheless

## Advanced Tips

1. **Speed Running**: Salt Plains + Summer + Traveler's Grace = fastest route
2. **Safe Route**: High Road + Spring + Iron Constitution = easiest journey
3. **Relic Collector**: Merchant + Light Burden = maximum carrying capacity
4. **Pure Faith**: Priest + Divine Protection = corruption resistance
5. **Balanced Approach**: Balanced deck + Scholar = versatility

## Common Mistakes

1. Ignoring sustenance until it's too late
2. Letting spirit drop below 40 (hand size penalty)
3. Carrying too many relics (overweight)
4. Never using shrines (corruption builds up)
5. Taking too many risks when low on health
6. Skipping all settlements (missing crucial trades)
7. Ignoring season effects when planning

## Win Conditions

To successfully reach and be accepted by the Magi:
1. Reach 1000 progress (arrive at Magi)
2. Maintain spirit above 70 (preferably)
3. Keep corruption below 20 (preferably)
4. Stay alive (health > 0, spirit > 0)

The journey is harsh. Many pilgrims fall. But those who plan well, manage resources carefully, and maintain their faith have a chance to reach the holy city.

Good luck, pilgrim. The Magi awaits.
