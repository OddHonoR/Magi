# Gameplay Guide

## Overview

Magi: Deck Builder CYOA is a card-based adventure game where you build your deck of spells while making choices that shape your journey. Battle enemies using strategic card play and overcome challenges to defeat the Dark Sorcerer!

## Core Mechanics

### Deck Building

Your deck is your collection of cards. You start with a basic deck and add powerful cards as you progress.

**Starting Deck:**
- 5x Strike (Deal 6 damage, Cost: 1 energy)
- 5x Defend (Gain 5 block, Cost: 1 energy)

**Card Rewards:**
- After each victory, you can choose to add a new card to your deck
- Choose cards that complement your strategy
- You can also skip adding a card if you want to keep your deck lean

### Combat System

Combat is turn-based. Each turn:

1. **Your Turn:**
   - Draw 5 cards from your deck
   - Start with your maximum energy (default: 3)
   - Play cards by clicking them
   - Each card costs energy to play
   - End your turn when ready

2. **Enemy Turn:**
   - The enemy executes their intent
   - Enemy intents are displayed above them
   - Plan your strategy based on what they'll do

3. **New Turn:**
   - Your block resets to 0
   - Your energy is restored
   - Discard your hand
   - Draw 5 new cards

### Card Types

**Attack Cards (Red)**
- Deal damage to enemies
- Examples: Strike, Fireball, Heavy Strike

**Defense Cards (Blue)**
- Gain block to prevent damage
- Block lasts until the start of your next turn
- Examples: Defend, Shield Barrier, Iron Defense

**Skill Cards (Green)**
- Special effects like drawing cards or gaining energy
- Examples: Meditation, Quick Strike, Dodge

### Resources

**HP (Hit Points)**
- Your life total
- If it reaches 0, you lose
- Can be restored by resting or healing

**Energy**
- Used to play cards
- Resets to maximum each turn
- Default maximum: 3

**Block**
- Prevents damage
- Resets to 0 at the start of your turn
- Can stack multiple block cards

### Deck Cycling

**Draw Pile:**
- Cards you haven't drawn yet
- Shuffled at the start of combat

**Hand:**
- Cards you can play this turn
- Maximum of 5 cards drawn per turn

**Discard Pile:**
- Cards you've already played or discarded
- When draw pile is empty, discard pile is shuffled back in

## Strategy Tips

### General Strategy

1. **Balance Your Deck**
   - Mix attack and defense cards
   - Don't make your deck too large
   - Every card should serve a purpose

2. **Watch Enemy Intent**
   - If enemy will attack for high damage, prioritize block
   - If enemy has low intent, focus on dealing damage
   - Plan 1-2 turns ahead when possible

3. **Energy Management**
   - Don't always play all your cards
   - Sometimes it's better to save energy for key cards
   - Consider card costs when building your deck

4. **Card Synergies**
   - Look for cards that work well together
   - Cards that draw more cards can help you find answers
   - Energy-generating cards let you play more in one turn

### Combat Tactics

**Early Turns:**
- Establish some block to stay safe
- Deal consistent damage to reduce enemy turns
- Don't overspend energy on overkill

**Mid Combat:**
- Adapt to enemy patterns
- Use your best cards when they matter most
- Maintain a sustainable cycle of offense and defense

**Finishing Moves:**
- Calculate lethal damage
- Don't waste block when you can win
- Use all available energy for the killing blow

### Deck Building Tips

**Card Selection:**
- Higher power cards aren't always better
- Consider card cost relative to your energy
- Low-cost cards give you flexibility
- High-cost cards must provide enough value

**Deck Composition:**
- Aim for 60-70% offensive cards
- 30-40% defensive/utility cards
- Remove weak cards when possible (if game allows)

## Story Choices

The game includes CYOA elements where your choices matter:

- **Character Specialization**: Choose your starting bonus
- **Path Decisions**: Affect which enemies you face
- **Risk vs. Reward**: Some choices offer different challenges

## Encounters

### Goblin Scout
- HP: 30
- Attack: 6
- Strategy: Basic enemy, good for learning mechanics

### Wolf Pack / Hill Troll
- HP: 45-50
- Attack: 8-10
- Strategy: Tougher fight, requires good deck

### Dark Sorcerer (Boss)
- HP: 80
- Attack: 12
- Strategy: Final challenge, needs strong deck and good play

## Progression

1. **Early Game**: Learn basic mechanics, build foundation
2. **Mid Game**: Add powerful cards, refine strategy
3. **Late Game**: Face the boss with your complete deck

## Win Conditions

- Defeat the Dark Sorcerer
- Keep your HP above 0
- Use your deck effectively

## Lose Conditions

- HP reaches 0
- Can restart from beginning

## Tips for Success

1. **Learn Enemy Patterns**: Each enemy has predictable behavior
2. **Don't Be Greedy**: Sometimes skipping a card reward keeps your deck efficient

4. **Experiment**: Try different strategies and card combinations
5. **Plan Ahead**: Think about what cards you'll draw next
