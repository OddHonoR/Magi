# Card Reference

This document lists all cards available in Magi: Deck Builder CYOA.

## Starting Deck

These cards are in your deck at the beginning of the game:

| Card Name | Type | Cost | Effect | Description |
|-----------|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Strike | Attack | 1 | Deal 6 damage | Basic attack card |
| Defend | Defense | 1 | Gain 5 block | Basic defense card |

**Starting Deck Composition:**
- 5x Strike
- 5x Defend
- **Total: 10 cards**

## First Encounter Rewards

Defeat the Goblin Scout to choose one:

| Card Name | Type | Cost | Effect | Description |
|-----------|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Heavy Strike | Attack | 2 | Deal 12 damage | High damage attack |
| Iron Defense | Defense | 2 | Gain 10 block | Strong defensive card |
| Swift Strike | Attack | 0 | Deal 4 damage | Free damage |

**Strategy Tips:**
- **Heavy Strike**: Good if you want more damage output
- **Iron Defense**: Choose if you're struggling to survive
- **Swift Strike**: Free cards are always powerful, gives flexibility

## Second Encounter Rewards

Defeat the Wolf Pack or Hill Troll to choose one:

| Card Name | Type | Cost | Effect | Description |
|-----------|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Power Strike | Attack | 3 | Deal 15 damage | Highest single-target damage |
| Dodge | Defense | 1 | Gain 6 block, Draw 1 card | Block + card draw |
| Meditation | Skill | 1 | Gain 2 energy next turn | Energy generation |

**Strategy Tips:**
- **Power Strike**: Expensive but powerful finisher
- **Dodge**: Versatile card that provides defense and deck cycling
- **Meditation**: Enables powerful combo turns with extra energy

## Card Combinations

### Aggressive Combos

**Energy Burst Turn:**
- Turn 1: Play Meditation
- Turn 2: Have 5 energy to unleash multiple attacks

**Free Damage:**
- Swift Strike + any other cards = maximize damage output

### Defensive Combos

**Block Stack:**
- Multiple defense cards in one turn
- Especially effective before high enemy damage

**Dodge Cycling:**
- Dodge draws a card, potentially drawing more blocks or attacks

### Balanced Combos

**Quick Strike Loop:**
- Quick Strike draws a card, potentially drawing another Quick Strike
- Provides damage while cycling through deck

## Deck Archetypes

### Aggressive Deck
Focus on attack cards with some minimal defense:
- 3-4 Defend cards
- 6-8 Attack cards
- 1-2 Utility cards

### Defensive Deck
Focus on surviving with block:
- 5-6 Defense cards
- 4-5 Attack cards
- 1-2 Utility cards

### Balanced Deck
Mix of both with some utility:
- 4-5 Defense cards
- 5-6 Attack cards
- 1-2 Utility cards

## Card Acquisition Strategy

### First Reward (After Goblin)
**If low HP:** Iron Defense helps survival
**If healthy:** Heavy Strike accelerates future fights
**General:** Swift Strike is always good value

### Second Reward (After Wolf/Troll)
**Aggressive:** Power Strike for boss burst damage
**Need cycling:** Dodge helps find the cards you need
**Long game:** Meditation enables big combo turns

## Advanced Card Concepts

### Deck Thinning
- Smaller deck = see your best cards more often
- Only take cards that improve your deck
- Sometimes skipping rewards is correct

### Energy Curve
- Balance low-cost and high-cost cards
- Need to be able to spend energy efficiently each turn
- Too many 3-cost cards = wasted energy

### Card Draw
- Cards that draw are powerful
- Help you find answers to threats
- Quick Strike and Dodge provide this

## Enemy HP Thresholds

Knowing when you can finish enemies is crucial:

**Goblin Scout (30 HP):**
- 5 Strikes = 30 damage (5 energy needed over multiple turns)
- 3 Strikes + 1 Fireball = 27 damage (close enough)

**Wolf Pack / Troll (45-50 HP):**
- Needs ~8-9 Strike equivalents
- 3-4 turn fight typically

**Dark Sorcerer (80 HP):**
- Needs ~13-14 Strike equivalents
- 5-7 turn fight typically
- Must balance offense and defense
